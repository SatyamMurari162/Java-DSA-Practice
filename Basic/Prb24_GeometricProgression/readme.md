P25. Find the Sum of a Geometric Progression

Problem: Find the sum of the first 6 terms of a geometric progression with first term 3 and common ratio 2 (i.e., 3, 6,
12, 24...).

Approach:
Use the GP sum formula: S = a(rn-1)/(r-1) when r„1, avoiding the need to generate and add every term individually for
large n.
