P7. Sum of Digits Until Single Digit (Digital Root)

Problem: Repeatedly sum the digits of 9875 until only a single digit remains.

To find the digital root of 9875:

Add the digits:
9 + 8 + 7 + 5 = 29
Since 29 has two digits, add again:
2 + 9 = 11
Since 11 has two digits, add again:
1 + 1 = 2
Answer: 2
